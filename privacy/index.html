Bengali Panjika Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Bengali Panjika (বাংলা পঞ্জিকা) is published by Focustech.

Summary

The app has no accounts, ads or analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information kept on your device

Your city, language, reminder choices, Hijri adjustment and optional birth rashi and nakshatra are stored only on your device. They are used to calculate the panjika, reminders and home-screen widget, and are never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Internet use

The app downloads public festival, muhurta and holiday date files from GitHub Pages. These requests contain none of your app settings. As with any website, the hosting provider may process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the file. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Permissions

  • Notifications: festival, Ekadashi, parana, moon and vrat reminders you turn on.
  • Alarms and reminders: delivers those reminders on time.
  • Run at startup: restores reminders and widget updates after a restart.
  • Internet: downloads updated date files.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the day's panjika goes to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
